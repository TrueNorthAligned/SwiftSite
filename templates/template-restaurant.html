Our Story

Bella Napoli brings the authentic flavors of Southern Italy to Charleston. Founded by the Rossi family, our recipes have been passed down through three generations — from the hills of Naples to your table.

We source the freshest local ingredients and import our olive oil, cheeses, and wines directly from Italy. Every dish is made from scratch, with love and tradition in every bite.
