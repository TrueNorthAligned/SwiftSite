Care You Can Trust

Coastal Dental Care has been the trusted family dentist in Charleston for over two decades. We combine advanced dental technology with a gentle, personalized approach to make every patient feel at ease.

From toddlers to grandparents, we provide comprehensive dental care for every stage of life. Our state-of-the-art facility offers digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and same-day crowns — all in a relaxing, spa-like setting.

Dental Services

Comprehensive care for every smile — from routine to restorative.

🦷

General Dentistry

Cleanings, exams, fillings, and preventive care. The foundation of a healthy smile — recommended every six months.

✨

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and smile makeovers. Transform your smile and boost your confidence.

🦷

Orthodontics

Invisalign clear aligners and traditional braces for all ages. Straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably.

🏗️

Crowns & Bridges

Same-day crowns, bridges, and inlays using CEREC technology. Restore damaged teeth in a single visit.

🦷

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement that looks and feels natural. Single implants, implant bridges, and All-on-4.

Emergency Care

Toothaches, chipped teeth, lost fillings, and dental trauma. Same-day emergency appointments available.
