About Our Firm

Rodriguez & Associates has been a pillar of the Charleston legal community for over two decades. Founded by Maria Rodriguez, our firm is built on a foundation of integrity, diligence, and a deep commitment to our clients.

We believe in personalized legal service — every client works directly with an experienced attorney, not just a paralegal. We take the time to understand your unique situation and craft a strategy tailored to your goals.
